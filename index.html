Before the Frost

You are one small sparrow, and winter is coming. Alone you will never survive the journey south — gather a flock before the first frost, then lead it across the river.

1 · Find the flocks. Golden beams mark the old oaks where sparrows roost. Fly close and press E to perch.
2 · Befriend the alpha. Watch the birds, read the field note, and chirp at the leader. Win the alpha and its whole flock follows you. Guess wrong twice and they scatter.
3 · Keep them together. A hungry flock deserts you — forage at berry bushes and sunflowers. Call back distracted wanderers with E, and hide from hawks under the trees.
4 · Fly south. Once your flock is big enough, cross the river on the southern edge of the map before the clock runs out.
